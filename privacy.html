Privacy & Disclaimer

Policies, publication guidance and information about this website.

About The Voice Pipe

The Voice Pipe shares news, history, traditions and contributions associated with HMCS Tecumseh. This website provides public access to published editions and related material. It is maintained in support of the unit; it is not a substitute for official Royal Canadian Navy, Canadian Armed Forces or Department of National Defence communications.

For authoritative information, consult the Department of National Defence and the Royal Canadian Navy.

Editorial Disclaimer

Articles may include personal accounts, recollections and opinions. Unless expressly identified as an official statement, the views expressed are those of their contributors and do not necessarily represent the policies or positions of HMCS Tecumseh, the Royal Canadian Navy, the Canadian Armed Forces or the Department of National Defence.

Material is prepared with care, but historical accounts, third-party submissions and other content may contain errors or become outdated. Publication does not constitute official endorsement of a contributor, organization, product or service.

Information Security and Public Release

This is a public website. Only material suitable for public distribution should be submitted or published. Contributors must not provide classified, protected, operationally sensitive or otherwise restricted information. Approval to publish must be obtained through applicable channels before material is posted.

Relevant guidance: DND/CAF policies and standards and DND publications and reports. Members should follow current unit direction and applicable orders; this page does not replace them.

Privacy and Personal Information

Published issues may contain names, ranks, service histories, photographs or other identifying details provided for publication. Contributors should obtain appropriate permission before submitting information or images about other people, especially minors. Do not submit private contact details, medical information or other sensitive personal information for public posting.

Requests to correct or remove personal information from this website should be raised through the HMCS Tecumseh editorial or unit point of contact. Government of Canada guidance: DND privacy, Access to Information and Privacy, and the Privacy Act. Those resources describe government obligations and do not, by themselves, describe this website’s own data-handling practices.

Website Analytics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic and performance. Cloudflare describes its Web Analytics service as privacy-focused and designed without visitor-tracking cookies. The website does not use analytics to identify individual readers.

For information about the service and Cloudflare’s handling of data, see Cloudflare Web Analytics documentation and Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Visiting linked external websites may involve separate data collection governed by their own policies.

Copyright and Attribution

Text, photographs, illustrations and archival material may have different copyright owners or usage restrictions. Inclusion in The Voice Pipe does not transfer ownership or grant permission for unrestricted reuse. Credit and source information are provided where available; any omissions may be brought to the editorial team’s attention.

Before reproducing material, confirm the rights and permissions that apply. See the Copyright Act and Canadian Heritage copyright information.

External Links

Links are provided for convenience and reference. External websites are operated by their respective owners and may change without notice. A link does not imply endorsement by HMCS Tecumseh, the Royal Canadian Navy, the Canadian Armed Forces or the Department of National Defence. Review the external site’s terms and privacy practices before providing information.

Corrections and Contact

To report an error, raise a privacy or copyright concern, or request a correction to published material, contact the Voice Pipe editorial team through established HMCS Tecumseh unit channels. Please identify the issue, article or page and describe the requested correction. Do not include sensitive personal or operational information in an initial enquiry.

For official unit enquiries, consult the Naval Reserve information page or the Department of National Defence contact directory.
